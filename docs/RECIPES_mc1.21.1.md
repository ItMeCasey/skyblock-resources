# Recipes for other mods: NeoForge 1.21.1

Files: `overrides/1.21.1-neoforge/data/skyblock_resources/recipe/{plants,compat}/<modid>/...` (1875 recipe files plus 4 `.delete` markers). Written once from a scan of the NeoForge builds, committed as plain data, same rules as `docs/PLANT_RECIPE_RULES.md`. Nothing here was taken from `overrides/1.21.1-forge` or the Forge scan.

## Method

- Scratch copy of `versions/mc1.21.1/neoforge` under `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Temp\skyres-scan-neoforge-1.21.1\proj` (the repo project was not touched, no scanner is in it). Scan server on port 8914 for the game test runs.
- Mods loaded (Modrinth, loader `neoforge`, Minecraft 1.21.1, jar has `neoforge.mods.toml`), with their libraries: Regions Unexplored, Oh The Biomes We've Gone, Nature's Spirit, Ecologics, Occultism, Mystical Agriculture (+ Agradditions), Forbidden Arcanus, EvilCraft, Integrated Dynamics, Ex Deorum, AE2, Undergarden, Create (+ Create Dragons Plus, Create Integrated Farming), Quark, Firmalife (+ TerraFirmaCraft, ArborFirmaCraft), Farmer's Delight, Ube's Delight, Expanded Delight, Aether, Ars Nouveau, Immersive Engineering, Atmospheric, Environmental, Upgrade Aquatic, Autumnity, Deeper and Darker, Galosphere, Gardens of the Dead, Botany Pots, Promenade, Bountiful Fares, Pam's HarvestCraft 2 Crops and Trees, [Let's Do] Meadow and BloomingNature, Eidolon Repraised, Productive Trees, Ad Astra, Expanded Ecosphere, Croptopia 4.2.4 (CurseForge), Biomes O' Plenty 21.1.0.14. All of them load together in one dev server (52 jars).
- A scanner on `ServerStartedEvent` listed every block item. Trees were grown for real (bonemeal, then `SaplingBlock.advanceTree`, on the first few soils where the sapling survives, single then 2x2) and the trunk and leaf blocks found; trunk loot once plus leaf loot 20 times, 500 rolls, empty tool (min, max, chance per item). Plants were sampled with `BlockState.getDrops` at full growth, 500 rolls. Resource blocks: the drop with a netherite pickaxe must be the block itself.
- Scans were run in batches of mods; the scan server is the vanilla game test server type (`gameTestServer`), which does not read an `eula.txt`. No `eula.txt` was created or edited.
- Files are generated by a script (scratch directory), then checked: no air, no zero amounts, first drop equals `outputs`, every non-`minecraft` drop mod is in the conditions, no duplicate recipe key (type, item, soil) against `common/`, `overrides/1.21.1` and this folder.
- A generated file whose plant already has a `common/` recipe with the same drop ids is not written: 486 files (all of Croptopia and Mystical Agriculture, most Biomes O' Plenty, Nature's Spirit, Ecologics, Evilcraft, Occultism, Forbidden Arcanus). Where the ids differ the common file path is overwritten here (18 files): BOP flowering_oak_sapling (now also drops its own sapling), Integrated Dynamics menril_sapling (no crystalized chunk in this build), the four Nature's Spirit wisteria saplings.

## Replacements and deletes for `common/`

- `.delete` markers (4): `compat/create/{stone,iron,diamond,netherite}_weathered_limestone_generation.json`. `create:weathered_limestone` does not exist in the NeoForge 1.21.1 Create build (the server logged a parse error for these; no other `skyblock_resources` recipe fails).
- The 18 replaced files above.

## Not covered (and why)

- Twilight Forest, Allthemodium: CurseForge only; no file id could be resolved without the CurseForge API, so not loaded. Croptopia is loaded (file id already pinned in the project).
- TerraFirmaCraft itself (loaded only because Firmalife and ArborFirmaCraft need it): not in the matrix, no recipes. Its logs appear as drops of ArborFirmaCraft trees (conditions list `afc` and `tfc`).
- Mystical Agradditions, Botany Pots, Create, Create Integrated Farming, Create Dragons Plus, AE2, Ex Deorum, Expanded Ecosphere, Terralith, Oh The Trees You'll Grow: no plants or no new raw blocks (Botany Pots and Ex Deorum only add machine or sieve material blocks that are crafted).
- Not on NeoForge 1.21.1 per the matrix (`-`): Alex's Caves, Tinkers' Construct, Blue Skies, Biome Makeover, Ex Nihilo Sequentia, Botania, Vintage Delight, Thermal Cultivation, Wilder Wild. The `exnihilosequentia` compat files in `common/` stay inert.
- Items that did not grow or have no usable drops in this build: BWG cypress and spirit saplings, BloomingNature baobab sapling and Bountiful Fares coconut (no tree grew, even with `advanceTree` and every soil), NS bitter sprouts, Soulblight sprouts, Firmalife herbs and pineapple/nightshade bushes (empty drops).

## Judgment calls

- Left out as decoration: shrubs, branches, beards, leaf litter and piles, flower piles, vines, ivy, roots, sprouts, ferns, grasses and anything whose only loot is wheat or beetroot seeds, lichens, clusters, colonies, garden blocks, fruit pods (Ars Nouveau), hanging fruit, `*_natural` variants, Pam's fruit blocks.
- Produce items that share the seed's plant (96 Pam's `*item` crops, `blockItemIsDefault` false) are skipped; the `*seeditem` is the recipe. Pam's brussels sprout seed was scanned separately (its item name looked like a sapling to the scanner).
- Wild crops (Farmer's Delight, Expanded Delight, Ube's Delight, Bountiful Fares, Firmalife) are kept when they give something new; wild wheat, carrots, potatoes and beetroots are skipped (vanilla loot only).
- Nether fungi of Promenade and Gardens of the Dead grew, but are written like vanilla crimson fungus: a crop on netherrack that drops itself.
- Pam's fruit tree saplings grow vanilla oak, jungle or spruce trees; the fruit blocks are not rolled, so the recipes give vanilla logs, saplings, sticks and apples. Atmospheric currant seedling grows a stalk, not a log.
- Plants that only live on special ground and have no dirt soil (Deeper and Darker glowing flowers, gloomy cactus, ice lily; Forbidden Arcanus fungyss and growing edelwood; Quark glow shroom; Galosphere cordyceps) are out. RU small oak and kapok saplings (did not grow as trees) and the saguaro flower are out.
- Fire and water gating, hunger and seasons of the mods are not modeled; drops are full-growth loot only. Farmer's Delight tomato seeds give only the seed at full growth (the vine is a second block).
- `common/.../tags/item/wooden_tree_items.json` typo (`biomeswebegone_saplings`) noted in the Forge doc is still in `common/`.
